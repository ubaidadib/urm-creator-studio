# URM Creator Studio — English Guide

**URM Creator Studio** helps you make professional, branded videos just by chatting. You do not need
any experience with AI or video editing. You describe the video you want, share your photos and clips,
and the assistant does the editing on your own computer — with your logo, your colours and your fonts.

The studio has first-class Arabic support: Arabic letters always come out connected and in the right
order, which many video tools get wrong. You can work fully in English, fully in Arabic, or in both.

> URM Creator Studio is shared for free by URM ENROLL with its social-media followers.
> Your videos always carry **your own** brand. URM ENROLL's name or logo never appears in them.

## The short version

- **Install once:** double-click the installer for your computer (see [Installation](02-installation.md)).
- **Open Claude Code** and type: `Hi, I want to make a video`.
- **Answer one simple question at a time.** If you are unsure, say "choose for me".
- **Put your files** in the `URM-Creator-Studio/inbox` folder in your home folder, or drag them into the chat.
- **Approve a quick test version**, then get the final video.
- **Find your video** in `URM-Creator-Studio/projects/<project name>/output/` — each attempt gets its own
  numbered folder (`v001`, `v002`, …).
- **You publish it yourself.** The assistant never posts anything for you.

## Words used in these guides

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Claude Code** | The AI assistant app you chat with. The studio runs inside it. |
| **Skill** | An add-on that teaches Claude Code a new job. URM Creator Studio is a skill. |
| **Workspace** | The `URM-Creator-Studio` folder in your home folder. Your brands, projects and finished videos live here. |
| **Brand profile** | Your saved name, logo, colours, fonts and social handles, reused for every video. |
| **Project** | One video you are working on. Each project has its own folder. |
| **Preview** | A quick, smaller test version of the video for you to check before the final one. |
| **Version** | Each time the video is put together, it is saved as a new version (`v001`, `v002`…). Older versions are kept. |
| **Captions / subtitles** | Text of what is being said, shown on the video. |

## Your files are safe

The studio **never changes, moves or renames your original photos, videos or music**. It works on
copies inside the project folder, and every new export goes into a new folder. If something goes
wrong, your originals are untouched.
