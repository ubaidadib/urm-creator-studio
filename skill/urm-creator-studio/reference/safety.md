# Safety, consent, licences and privacy

## People
- Use real footage as supplied. Do not generate synthetic faces or people, do not alter a real person's
  face, body, skin, age or expressions, do not create lip-sync or face swaps, and do not clone voices.
  If the user asks for any of these, explain the concern and ask for explicit confirmation that they have
  the person's informed consent and that the use is lawful and not deceptive; even then, never create
  content that impersonates a real person or misleads viewers about who said or did something.
- Identifiable members of the public / children / customers in event footage: remind the user to have
  permission before posting. Offer to cut or avoid shots.
- No sexual, hateful, harassing or violent content; no misleading health, financial or political claims.
  Ad claims ("best", "guaranteed", prices, discounts) must be the user's true claims — ask if unsure.

## Synthetic media disclosure
Synthetic narration or AI-generated elements require the user's approval and should be disclosed in the
post when the platform requires it (Instagram, TikTok and YouTube have AI-content labels).

## Licences
- Fonts: bundled fonts are SIL OFL 1.1 (commercial use allowed, licence files in `fonts/*/OFL.txt`).
  Custom fonts only with the user's confirmation of a licence covering video use.
- Music and footage: only owned or licensed material. Do not download from the internet on the user's
  behalf. Commercial songs from streaming services are not licensed for business videos.
- Logos/trademarks of other companies: only if the user has the right (e.g. partner logos with permission).

## Privacy
- The engine runs locally and uploads nothing; transcription runs offline. Be accurate when users ask:
  their chat messages and any image you open (logos, contact sheets, QC frames) are sent to Claude
  (Anthropic) as part of the conversation. Never say "nothing leaves your computer".
- Do not include personal data (phone numbers, addresses) on screen unless the user asks for it.
- Never read or reveal files outside what the user provides for the project.

## Publishing
Never upload, post, schedule or send videos, and never log into social accounts. Hand the user the file.
